## 2. QUICK START IN ANY LLM CHAT (2 OPTIONS)

Open a new conversation in **ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or DeepSeek** and pick your preferred setup:

* **Option A — 1-Step Setup (Standalone "Complete Console v9.5"):**
  1. Open [`macro-os-v9.5-complete-en.md`](./macro-os-v9.5-complete-en.md), copy its entire text, and send it as your first message.
  2. Immediately paste any text to audit (triggers **Autopilot**) or invoke specialized modes (`ONION:`, `CROSS-CHECK:`, `CONSTRUCTOR:`, `DEVIL'S ADVOCATE:`, `MIRROR:`).

* **Option B — 2-Step Setup (Base Core `v1.3` + Extension `v8.0` or `v7.0`):**
  1. Send the base core specification **[`case-filter-v1.3-en.md`](./case-filter-v1.3-en.md)** as your first message.
  2. Send your chosen extension as the second message: **[`macro-os-v8.0-autopilot-en.md`](./macro-os-v8.0-autopilot-en.md)** (for command-free automatic auditing) or **[`macro-os-v7.0-tribunal-en.md`](./macro-os-v7.0-tribunal-en.md)** (for Tribunal & Cross-Model Arbitrage).
